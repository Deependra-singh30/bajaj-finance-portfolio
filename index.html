Our Journey of Innovation

Milestones Shaping India's Financial Landscape

From a focused captive two-wheeler financier to India's most diversified retail fintech giant.

1987

Inception & Incorporation

Founded as Bajaj Auto Finance Ltd, initially financing two and three-wheeler vehicles across India.

2008

Retail NBFC Transformation

Rebranded to Bajaj Finance Limited; pioneered zero-cost consumer durable financing and SME loans.

2014

EMI Network Card Revolution

Crossed 10 million active customers and introduced the digital EMI card network, redefining retail checkout.
